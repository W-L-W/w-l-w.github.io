I worked with Edward and Jason to derive and implement a new RLHF algorithm, KLQ. It worked alright, but did not outperform PPO, and instead had strikingly similar behaviour. We followed up mathematically and found KLQ was in fact equivalent to a certain version of PPO, in a certain sense. The maths is intriguing. Read this if you want to dig into the details of how to operationalise RL for LLMs.
