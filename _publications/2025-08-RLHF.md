We implemented a new RLHF algorithm, and it worked alright, but did not outperform PPO, but seemed to behave very similarly. We then investigated this and found it was in fact equivalent to a certain version of PPO, in a certain sense. Read this if you want to dig into the details of how to operationalise RL for LLMs.
