This paper was spotlighted at the Learn to Compress Workshop at ISIT 2024. The work examines fundamental questions about the number of samples needed to for *Approximate Channel Simulation*, a new and little-studied paradigm in information theory that is the basis of (my good friend) [Greg's](https://gergely-flamich.github.io) PhD work.
